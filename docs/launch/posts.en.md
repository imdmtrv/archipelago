## 4. Reddit r/IndieDev

**Title:**
Balancing decisions over luck: using a bot simulator to tune my cozy financial browser game

**Body:**
I wanted to build a game where players experience a fundamental idea: wealth is not determined by the size of your salary, but by the gap between income and expenses, and what you do with that surplus.

There is the classic board game Cashflow, but in it the dice move the outcome more than the player's decisions do. When creating Archipelago, my cozy browser game about money, my primary goal was to ensure that decisions always take precedence over chance.

You begin on a small island with one of four professions: Lighthouse keeper (low salary, low expenses), Teacher (moderate salary and living costs), Ferry captain (high salary with steep costs), or Doctor (high income burdened by an initial student loan). Each turn represents one week of salary, living expenses, and market decisions. You can invest in productive assets like a fishing boat, smokehouse, or rental cottage, or spend money on upkeep-heavy status items like a yacht or golden owner statue.

To balance the economy without relying on pure luck, I built a bot simulator that runs thousands of games with bots that play different strategies. Every rule change goes through it: the goal is that choices, not luck, decide the outcome, and confirm that no profession has an unfair advantage when navigating storms, layoffs, neighbor loans with Mia, Timur, and Boris, or pyramid schemes like a "pearl farm" promising +25% a week. Once passive income covers expenses, players reach financial freedom and can build a round-the-world schooner.

The game is free, ad-free, requires no registration, and runs in the browser on desktop and mobile:
https://imdmtrv.github.io/archipelago/en/?utm_source=reddit

I would love to hear how other developers approach balancing systemic economic games with simulation testing.

## 5. itch.io Page

**Tagline:**
A cozy browser game about money, assets, and financial freedom.

**Description:**
Archipelago is a cozy browser game about money and freedom. You start on a small island with a job and a weekly paycheck. Your goal is simple: invest your surplus earnings into assets that generate passive income until that income covers all your living expenses. Once you achieve financial freedom, you can step away from work and build your dream round-the-world schooner.

Watch out for tempting liabilities that drain your cash on upkeep, manage requests from neighbors Mia, Timur, and Boris, keep a safety buffer for sudden layoffs or storms, and steer clear of shady pyramid schemes promising unrealistic returns.

**Features:**
- **4 distinct professions:** Start as a Lighthouse keeper, Teacher, Ferry captain, or Doctor with a student loan.
- **Assets vs. liabilities:** Grow weekly cash flow with a fishing boat, smokehouse, or rental cottage, while avoiding money drains like a yacht or a golden owner statue.
- **Living neighbors:** Mia, Timur, and Boris live their own financial lives, ask for loans, hold emergency fire sales, and share island rumors.
- **Real-world financial challenges:** Weather storms with insurance, protect against layoffs with a safety buffer, and recognize 3 pyramid schemes like the "pearl farm" (+25% a week).
- **Post-freedom dream:** Construct a round-the-world schooner stage by stage on your slipway once your passive income covers all costs.
- **Challenge friends:** Share your island link so friends can play the exact same setup and see who reaches freedom faster.
- **Accessible:** Free, no ads, no sign-up, plays directly in your browser on phone or desktop.

**How to Play:**
1. Choose your profession and begin on your island.
2. Each week, collect your salary and pay living expenses.
3. Review the deal board: buy assets that pay weekly passive income, and avoid status items that only incur upkeep costs.
4. Prepare for surprises by maintaining a cash safety buffer and purchasing insurance against storms and illnesses.
5. Reach financial freedom when passive income covers 100% of weekly expenses, then construct your round-the-world schooner on the shore slipway.
